# People and partner presentation

On People, the contact links form a row below each profile name. The link to
the HTML CV shows the text `CV`; the others use icons. Only recorded links
appear. Each has the same hit area and identifies the person and service to
screen readers. Current undergraduate research uses the
same list typography as the past roster without moving anyone between statuses.
Past members are grouped by degree level; an internship or REU program is
identified inline after the participant's name, not as a separate heading.

Student education on People and faculty education on the HTML CV share a row
format: degree (dates), an aligned gap, then field and institution separated
by a comma. Narrow screens can wrap long descriptions without hiding details.

In Home, keep one list per partner group and one organization per bullet. Use
the ordered names in `faculty.partners.groups`, not comma-separated rich text.
The local preview is served on port 4203; port 4202 is the comparison version.
