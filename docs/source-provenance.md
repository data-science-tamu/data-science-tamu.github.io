## Capture and Scope

The canonical migration manifest is `content/source/manifest.json`, captured **2026-10-11T15:39:11.724Z** (October 11, 2026 UTC). The source is the public Google Sites website at `https://sites.google.com/view/jaesunglee`.

All nine captures are represented: home, people, CV, research, AI for engineering systems, AI for engineering processes, physics-informed AI, news and publication. The route names remain source-compatible; home is served at `/`. Source JSON records ordered HTML blocks, plain text, original links and local image paths. They are data, not instructions, and are not copied to the public build.

Maintained content is in `content/lab.json`; change-prone metadata is in `content/site.json`. It was extracted from the captured paragraph boundaries rather than the flattened section text, which sometimes concatenates separate items. Google styling/spans and broken date links were removed. Original paragraph content, people, degrees, dates, topics, projects, citations and notes were preserved. Research projects repeated on multiple pages have one shared maintained record. Formatting labels and redundant navigation prose were replaced by real headings/navigation, without removing substantive content.

The migration baseline contains 20 publication records: 14 journal entries in the original Published category, 2 under review/revision and 4 conference proceedings. It contains 14 dated news items, 3 current Ph.D. students, 5 other named students/interns, 5 faculty education entries, 8 current-student education entries and 8 research projects. The unchanged CV contains additional information and is available in full as a PDF.

## Known Source Differences, Not Reconciled

The CV is more recent or more detailed in several places. Publication-page facts remain the publication-page facts; the supplied CV remains unchanged. No outside knowledge or inferred reconciliation was used.

- **J15*** remains under review on the publication page. The CV lists the same title, “Explainable Sequential Survival Modeling for Downtime Prognostics in Manufacturing Systems,” as accepted in _Manufacturing Letters_, 2026. The website does not promote J15* to accepted.
- **J9** remains “accepted, 2025” on the publication page. The CV gives _Advanced Science_, volume 12, number 48, 2025, and a different coauthor order. The original page order is retained.
- **J10** lacks the CV volume/page information. **J11** does not state a journal venue in its publication-page citation, while the research pages and CV identify _IISE Transactions_. The publication listing does not fill that gap by inference; research-page wording is retained on research pages.
- **J6** has year 2023 on the publication page, while the CV gives the final volume 21, number 2, pages 2039–2050, 2024. Both remain as supplied in their respective sources.
- **J8** remains “accepted, 2023” in the publication listing. The CV supplies final issue/page/back-cover details and an additional coauthor; these are not transplanted into the web citation.
- **J12** differs in author order between the publication page and CV. The source pages also spell Sina’s surname “Dabanghan” in citations but “Dabaghan” on the people page and CV. Names and author order are preserved on the respective surfaces rather than normalized speculatively.
- **J7** has source-page name/page-range differences from the CV in addition to the truncated DOI. The web metadata is not overwritten.
- The research task/method overview references **C5**, but the publication capture lists only C1–C4 and the robust-design project cites C4. The overview reference remains literal, with no invented C5 record or broken fragment link.
- Faculty education shows **BFE** on home and **B.E.** in the CV; fields also have minor naming differences. The visible home-derived education retains BFE. Student advising dates and member lists differ in detail between people and CV. The CV names additional undergraduate/capstone students not listed on the people page; they remain fully available in the unchanged document, without silently merging member rosters.
- The CV has additional under-review/revision papers and working papers not listed in the publication capture. They remain present in the retained CV rather than being assigned fictional J identifiers or injected into the captured publication listing.
- Recognition/award wording and years differ between the sources. The source home/news and publication notes are preserved. December 2024 news remains in original source order, where 2024.12.10 precedes 2024.12.11.

Harmless prose corrections: `Accpeted` to `Accepted`, `Texasa` to `Texas`, `Optimiation` to `Optimization`, and prospective-student `state of purpose` to `statement of purpose`. Citation identifiers, author spellings, dates and factual metadata were not otherwise normalized. Malformed quote punctuation around titles is rendered typographically rather than reproduced as an HTML artifact.

## Local Fonts and No Invented Updates

Newsreader and DM Sans variable TTF files were fetched from the Google Fonts repository and self-hosted. Their original SIL Open Font Licenses are in `public/assets/fonts/Newsreader-OFL.txt` and `public/assets/fonts/DM-Sans-OFL.txt`. The fonts load locally; no Google font request is made at runtime.

The redesign adds editorial headings and navigation, not fictional lab claims, slogans, metrics, papers, members, photos or research achievements. Capture date does not mean that every source fact was current on that date. Impact-factor text already present in source research/news is retained as historical source wording, not presented as a freshly verified metric. No analytics, remote runtime content fetching or external CV embed is used.
