## Evidence Boundaries

A paper DOI identifies an article; it does not by itself prove a best-paper
award, magazine coverage, top-viewed metric, or acceptance date. Likewise an
organization roster is not an election announcement, and a current editorial
board is not proof of an appointment start date. Local owner-supplied photos,
CV and original lab capture may support retained claims; those claims must not
be misrepresented as independently proved by an unrelated external link.

## Causal Boundary

Symptom: News and the shared Home/CV Montague award linked to the owner's
personal LinkedIn announcement instead of independently issued award evidence.
The grants record likewise linked the personal announcement despite the
existence of a department-owned announcement with the same facts.

The earliest documented source-selection decision for Montague is the
2025.11.06 row in docs/cv-news-refresh.md: the original refresh accepted the
owner announcement as a fallback after the indexed university announcement
returned 404. That approval then entered content/lab.json in both the News
record and faculty.awards, the shared source for Home and CV. This was a source
approval boundary, not a template or runtime transformation defect.

The contributing verification gap is visible in
tests/fixtures/cv-news-refresh.json: the exact personal href was encoded as an
approved after-value. tests/refresh-expectations.mjs correctly preserved that
value and rejected unrelated edits, but its contract checked the approved
bytes rather than independently validating the issuer or evidence scope.
Existing preservation tests therefore could not flag the accepted fallback as
a source-authority issue. A domain such as LinkedIn cannot itself distinguish
personal self-announcement from a university department or professional
organization's own announcement.

The correction is at that same boundary: the parent verified a named individual
TAMU award receipt and the department-issued grant announcement before the
content hrefs changed. New current-content tests pin those exact approved
issuers and reject personal News post links; the historical tests still retain
the originally approved fixture and hashes through a strict, explicit reversal.
No News date, award status, grant amount or publication fact is inferred from
the replacement pages or their publication dates.
